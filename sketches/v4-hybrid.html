🎭 Culture & Life

Vibe of the island.

Traditional dance, island flavors, and community celebrations that define Nauruan identity.

🕺

Dance & Music

Traditional Nauruan dance tells stories of the ocean, the harvest, and community life. Rhythmic movements passed down through generations.

🍛

Island Flavors

Coconut, fresh fish, breadfruit, and pandanus — Nauruan cuisine is pure Pacific. Try coconut-crusted reef fish.

🎉

Celebrations

Angam Day (Oct 26) celebrates population resilience. Constitution Day (May 17) is a week-long community festival.

📜 History

How we got here.

From ancient Micronesian settlement to independent republic — 3,000 years in four chapters.

~1000 BCE
First Settlers
Micronesian navigators arrive by outrigger canoe from the Caroline Islands.
1798
"Pleasant Island"
British Captain John Fearn spots Nauru, naming it for its welcoming shores.
1900
Phosphate Rush
Discovery of the world's richest phosphate deposits changes everything.
1968
Independence!
Nauru becomes the world's smallest independent republic on January 31.
